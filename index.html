SECTION_01 — INTRODUCTION

EVERYONE SAID
CREATIVE WORK
WAS SAFE.

For a while, that felt like a safe bet. AI was going to handle the boring stuff — factory floors, call centers, spreadsheets nobody wanted to touch. Not the creative work. Writing, illustration, music — those needed something a machine couldn't replicate. Taste. Lived experience. The ability to make something that actually meant something to another person.

Then 2022 happened and that whole assumption fell apart pretty fast. Midjourney, DALL-E, ChatGPT showed up and within a few months you could type a sentence and get back a finished illustration. A full ad campaign. A song. The line everyone thought existed just... wasn't there anymore.

That's kind of what I wanted to dig into with this project. Not just "AI is changing things" — that's obvious at this point — but specifically why companies are replacing creative workers so fast, what the actual numbers look like, and what it means for the people on the other side of it.
